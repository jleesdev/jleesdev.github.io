<!-- lang:en -->
Connect five or more identical pieces horizontally, vertically, or diagonally and they clear.
Pick a piece, pick an empty cell, and it walks there — but a blocked path is simply unavailable.
Every move spawns three new pieces and the game ends when there is nowhere to put them, so
deciding which cells to keep empty matters as much as building the next line.

- An `n × (n−4)` scoring curve that pays off steeply for bigger clears: five pieces score 5, eight score 32
- Consecutive clears stack a combo multiplier. Lucky clears, where spawned pieces complete a line on their own, and the chains that follow keep feeding it
- Undo costs one rewarded ad. A single bad move can collapse a board, which is the sharpest edge for new players
- Piece sets change how the pieces look, and hitting certain records unlocks new ones
