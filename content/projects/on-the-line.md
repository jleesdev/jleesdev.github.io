<!-- lang:en -->
A casual line puzzle: connect five or more identical objects horizontally, vertically, or
diagonally to clear them. Every move spawns three new objects, and the game ends when the
board fills up.

- Scoring design including combo multipliers and lucky clears
- Undo backed by AdMob rewarded ads
- Shipped and maintained on iOS
